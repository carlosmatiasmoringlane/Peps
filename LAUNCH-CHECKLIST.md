# Recheck before the site is public

Four items on this page carry risk that is different in kind from the rest of
the catalogue, plus two operational gaps. None of this is legal advice — it is
a list of what to put in front of someone who can give you that.

Raised 2026-09-22 and kept in the catalogue by decision. Re-read it before the
domain goes live, and again whenever `catalog.json` gains a product.

---

## 1. Somatropin — H10, H12

**21 U.S.C. § 333(e)** makes distributing human growth hormone for any use
other than an approved indication a federal offence, up to 5 years'
imprisonment (10 where the recipient is under 18).

This statute is unusual in that it does not carry the research-use exemption
that much of the rest of this market relies on. Treat it as separate from
every other line in the catalogue.

**Ask counsel specifically:** does our RUO labelling and buyer attestation
give us a defence under § 333(e), or does it not?

## 2. Tirzepatide, retatrutide, cagrilintide — TR*, RT*, CGL5

Under live patent protection (Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk). Both have pursued
sellers in volume. Tirzepatide is off the FDA shortage list, so the compounding
shortage exemption no longer applies.

**13 of 50 SKUs, and the highest-priced lines in the catalogue** — so this is a
commercial decision as much as a legal one. Confirm exposure before marketing
spend goes anywhere near them.

## 3. Bacteriostatic water — BA

Its only purpose is reconstituting peptides for injection into a person.
Listing it beside "for laboratory research use only" is the specific fact used
to argue the RUO label is pretextual — that the seller knew the intended use.

It is the cheapest line in the catalogue at $16. Weigh that against what it
costs the framing protecting the other 49.

## 4. § 02 and § 04 describe a business this catalogue is not

The page currently promises, for every lot:

- independent ISO/IEC 17025 re-assay
- a single audited manufacturer per sequence
- certificates published before a lot is listed
- cold-chain handling with a temperature record

That is not 50 SKUs dropshipped from three warehouses. **As written these
sections are false**, and they are the page's entire differentiator, so they
are also the first thing a serious buyer will test.

Either fund the programme or replace those sections with what actually
happens. A smaller true claim is worth more than a large one that does not
survive a question.
