# Episode 01 — Higgsfield Shot List

12 shots, 7:00 total. Timecodes match `../scripts/ep01-the-box.md`.
Style clause is identical in every prompt — that's intentional.

**Shared style clause** (appears in all prompts verbatim):
> Flat 2D vector animation, warm off-white background #F4F1EA, charcoal
> #1F1F1F line work, single accent orange #E2572B. Clean, minimal,
> museum-like. No text overlay. 16:9.

---

## S01 — [0:00-0:12] HOOK
> Flat 2D vector animation, warm off-white background #F4F1EA, charcoal
> #1F1F1F line work, single accent orange #E2572B. Clean, minimal,
> museum-like. No text overlay. 16:9.
> A single shipping container sits dead centre on an empty background,
> lit like a museum artifact on a plinth. Absolutely still for three
> seconds. Then it rotates slowly, exactly 90 degrees, and stops. The
> container is the only orange object in frame. Locked-off camera.

*Note: the stillness IS the hook. Resist adding motion.*

## S02 — [0:12-0:45] BREAK-BULK CHAOS
> [style clause]
> Cross-section of a 1940s cargo ship hold. Dozens of charcoal silhouette
> figures hand-stack mismatched cargo — sacks, barrels, crates of every
> different size, loose timber. Deliberately chaotic, no two objects
> alike, no grid or order anywhere. Continuous overlapping motion. Slow
> lateral camera drift left to right.

## S03 — [0:45-1:05] THE SHIP WAITS
> [style clause]
> Wide side view of a ship at a dock. A sun arcs across the sky behind it
> again and again, six times, speeding up. The ship does not move at all.
> Orange accent on the ship's hull only. Locked-off camera.

*The joke is that nothing happens. Hold it longer than feels right.*

## S04 — [1:05-1:50] THE COST IS AT THE EDGES
> [style clause]
> A horizontal route diagram: dock, then open ocean, then dock. The
> ocean segment is thin and pale. Both dock segments swell thick and
> orange, dwarfing the voyage between them. Animate the swelling from
> nothing. Locked-off camera.

## S05 — [1:50-2:20] McLEAN'S OBSERVATION
> [style clause]
> Side view: a truck parked beside a ship. Charcoal figures carry goods
> out of the truck and into the ship, one item at a time, looping.
> On the third loop the looping figures fade to 20% opacity and the path
> they walk illuminates in orange — revealing the redundant double
> handling. Locked-off camera.

## S06 — [2:20-2:40] IDEAL-X, 1956
> [style clause]
> A converted tanker at dock. One orange container lifts smoothly from a
> truck chassis and lands on the deck. Then the shot pulls back steadily
> to reveal 58 identical containers already aligned in a perfect grid.
> Smooth continuous pull-back. Single camera move, no cuts.

*The grid reveal is the emotional beat of the first half. Give it room.*

## S07 — [2:40-3:30] THE BOX ALONE DOESN'T WORK
> [style clause]
> One orange container at centre. Around it, five charcoal outline
> shapes appear in a ring: a crane, a ship slot, a truck chassis, a rail
> car, a foreign port. One by one, each shape tries to accept the
> container and visibly fails to fit — the container is too wide, too
> tall, the wrong shape each time. Each mismatch flashes briefly.
> Locked-off camera.

## S08 — [3:30-4:20] THE ARGUMENT
> [style clause]
> Four containers of visibly different dimensions sit in a row, each
> outlined in a different shade of charcoal. Measurement lines and
> dimension arrows extend from each, overlapping and conflicting in a
> tangle. The tangle slowly resolves as the mismatched boxes shrink away
> and two survive — one short, one exactly twice its length. Both turn
> orange. Locked-off camera.

*This is the thesis shot. The 1:2 ratio must read instantly.*

## S09 — [4:20-5:10] THE BOX AS INTERFACE
> [style clause]
> The same orange container passes in one unbroken horizontal motion
> through four charcoal silhouette systems: a crane, a ship, a train, a
> truck. Each system accepts it without pausing or adjusting. Perfectly
> smooth, mechanical, rhythmic — almost hypnotic. Camera tracks
> alongside at constant speed. Single continuous take.

## S10 — [5:10-5:55] THE HUMAN COST
> [style clause]
> A dense crowd of charcoal silhouette dockworkers fills the frame.
> One by one they fade out, from the edges inward, until the frame is
> empty except for a single automated crane moving alone. No orange
> accent anywhere in this shot. Slow, unhurried fades. Locked-off camera.

*The only shot in the episode with no accent colour. Deliberate — it
should feel colder. Music drops out here.*

## S11 — [5:55-6:40] THE PATTERN EVERYWHERE
> [style clause]
> Four objects appear in sequence in the same centre position, each
> replacing the last with a clean cut: a screw thread, a wooden pallet, a
> railway track gauge, an electrical plug. The first three settle
> cleanly into matching orange sockets. The fourth — the plug — does not
> fit its socket and sits there unmatched. Locked-off camera.

*The plug failing is the punchline. Hold two extra beats on it.*

## S12 — [6:40-7:00] OUTRO
> [style clause]
> Return to the exact opening shot: the single container centred on the
> plinth, completely still. Identical framing to S01. Hold. The channel
> wordmark fades in below it. Locked-off camera.

## Generation order

Do **S08** and **S01** first. S08 is the hardest shot and proves the
style works under load; S01 sets the framing that S12 must match exactly.
If those two land, the rest are variations.

## Consistency checklist

- [ ] Style clause byte-identical across all 12 prompts
- [ ] Exactly one orange element per shot (S10 has zero — intentional)
- [ ] S01 and S12 framing match precisely
- [ ] Container proportions identical in every shot it appears
- [ ] No camera zoom or shake anywhere in the episode
- [ ] One moving element at a time, except S02 (chaos is the point)
