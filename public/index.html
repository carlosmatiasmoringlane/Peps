§ 01 — Problem

Three things go wrong between the synthesiser and your bench.

None of them are exotic. They are the ordinary failure modes of a market where the paperwork is optional.

  • 01 / 03

    The COA arrives after the money does

    Most suppliers will send a certificate if you email and ask, and some of what comes back is a PDF for a different lot entirely. You cannot evaluate a reagent you have already paid for.

  • 02 / 03

    The lot number on the vial isn't on the certificate

    A purity figure that can't be tied to the vial in your hand is a marketing number. Traceability is a two-way link or it is nothing.

  • 03 / 03

    Nobody can tell you how it was stored

    Lyophilised peptide is stable; it stops being stable after a week on a loading dock in August. Without a handling record, the assay describes a material that no longer exists.

§ 04 — Chain of custody

How a lot reaches you.

We are not a synthesis house and we don't pretend to be. We qualify manufacturers, re-test what they send, and take responsibility for the number we publish.

  • Step 01

    Sourced from a qualified manufacturer

    Solid-phase synthesis at a facility we have audited, against a fixed specification and a single method per sequence. One manufacturer per sequence — we would rather carry less than reconcile two suppliers' chromatography.

  • Step 02

    Re-assayed independently on arrival

    Every lot is re-tested by an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory that has no commercial relationship with the manufacturer. We publish that lab's numbers, not the ones that came in the box.

  • Step 03

    Released, published, and shipped cold

    The COA goes into a public library indexed by lot number before the lot is listed for sale. Vials ship on gel packs with a temperature record you can read against the certificate.

§ 06 — Compliance

What these materials are, and are not.

For laboratory research use only. Peptra products are sold as research reagents for in-vitro and laboratory investigation by qualified personnel. They are not drugs, foods, cosmetics or dietary supplements.

Not for human or veterinary use. These materials are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and must not be administered to humans or animals. Peptra makes no therapeutic claim for any sequence it lists, publishes no dosing guidance, and will decline orders where the stated use is not research.

Buyer responsibilities. Purchasers must be affiliated with a research institution or business and are responsible for handling, storage, and compliance with the law in their own jurisdiction. Availability varies by country; some sequences cannot be shipped to some destinations.
